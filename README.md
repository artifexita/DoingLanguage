**DoingLanguage** is an accessible, evidence-based Progressive Web App designed to help individuals recovering from stroke, brain injury, apraxia, and aphasia rebuild their speech and language abilities.

While clinical therapy is essential, motor recovery and neuroplasticity require consistent, high-repetition practice between sessions. DoingLanguage bridges this gap with a structured 10-tier curriculum grounded in the Principles of Motor Learning—systematically guiding learners from individual phonemes and symbols up to complex sentences and expressive conversation.

Equipped with interactive speech recognition, natural voice synthesis, and adaptive multi-modal cueing, the app provides real-time, supportive feedback. Its patience-first interface removes timers, penalties, and pressure, featuring large touch targets and seamless offline capability so practice can happen anywhere.

Whether used as a supplementary home tool alongside speech-language pathology or for self-directed rehabilitation, DoingLanguage empowers users to practice at their own pace, rebuild language pathways, and reclaim their voice.
